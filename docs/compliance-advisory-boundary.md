# Compliance-Safe Advisory Boundary

## Positioning

This product is a research analytics platform. It is not a personalized investment advisory service.

## Required Product Copy

Use the following message in prediction/report surfaces:

"Research analytics only. Not personalized investment advice. Use with your own risk controls."

## Prohibited Claims

- "Guaranteed returns"
- "Always beats market"
- "This is financial advice for your portfolio"

## Safe Language Patterns

- "signal suggests"
- "model indicates"
- "validation status is warn/fail/pass"
- "use together with your own risk limits"

## Surface Checklist

1. Prediction page: disclaimer displayed.
2. AI explanation: wording avoids imperative investment instruction.
3. Export report: disclaimer embedded.
4. README/commercial docs: explicit advisory boundary.
