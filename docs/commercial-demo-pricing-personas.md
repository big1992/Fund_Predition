# Commercial Demo Flow, Pricing Tiers, and Personas

## Buyer Personas

1. Serious Retail Investor
- Needs risk-aware ideas, not black-box signals.
- Values downside/upside bands, validation status, and alerts.

2. Financial Advisor (Small Practice)
- Needs fast, explainable research packets for client meetings.
- Values exportable reports and compliance-safe copy.

3. Small Research Desk / Fund Boutique
- Needs repeatable workflow and model governance visibility.
- Values model registry, drift monitoring, and watchlist alerts.

## Demo Flow (15-20 min)

1. Collect data for a symbol universe.
2. Train models and show validation report card.
3. Open Prediction to show risk-first view:
- downside/base/upside
- model disagreement
- confidence band
- watchlist alerts
4. Open Model Performance:
- model registry/version history
- drift status and retrain suggestion
5. Export research report as CSV/PDF.
6. Close with compliance-safe boundary:
- research analytics, not personalized investment advice.

## Suggested Pricing Tiers

1. Starter (Free / Demo)
- Limited symbols
- Basic prediction and chart
- No exports

2. Pro (Paid)
- Full watchlist alerts
- CSV/PDF research exports
- Model validation + drift monitor views

3. Team (Paid+)
- Multi-user workflow
- Shared watchlists and scheduled report packs
- Priority support / custom model governance
